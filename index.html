Athanasia

Thai Literary Translator

Biography

Athanasia is a Thai literary translator specializing in direct translations of Russian literature from Russian into Thai.

Athanasia's translation approach focuses on preserving the linguistic structure, punctuation, stylistic features, and rhythm of the original Russian texts rather than adapting them into conventional Thai literary forms.

Translation Philosophy

Translated into Thai without making it Thai.

The translation method prioritizes fidelity to the source text, preserving wording, structure, symbols, punctuation, and the characteristics of the original language.
